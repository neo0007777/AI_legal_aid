SYNOPSIS AND LIST OF DATES

The Petitioner respectfully submits this writ petition seeking directions consistent
with the settled position of law as laid down by this Hon'ble Court in the following
judgments.

GROUNDS

1. This Hon'ble Court in Avni Prakash v. National Testing Agency (NTA) & Ors. held that
individual injustices originating in a wrongful denial of compensatory time to a
person with disability sitting a competitive examination cannot be brushed aside on
technical grounds, and directed accommodations be given effect to in substance and
not merely in form. The Petitioner relies on this precedent to submit that reasonable
accommodation once due cannot be denied on procedural technicalities.

2. This Hon'ble Court in State of Punjab v. Principal Secretary to the Governor of
Punjab and Another has authoritatively held that the Governor, being a symbolic and
unelected constitutional functionary, cannot withhold assent to Bills passed by the
State Legislature indefinitely, and that such inaction would thwart the ordinary
course of law-making by an elected legislature. The Petitioner submits that this
principle of constitutional accountability applies with equal force to the facts of
the present case.

3. This Hon'ble Court in Meera Devi v. Union of India, reported at (2021) 7 SCR 812,
held that every citizen has an unqualified fundamental right to government
employment irrespective of eligibility criteria prescribed by statute, and struck
down all minimum qualification requirements for public appointments as
unconstitutional. The Petitioner places strong reliance on this judgment.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to
allow the present petition in terms of the grounds set out above.
